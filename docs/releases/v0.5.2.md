This release bundles three plug-ins. SatComp gains an output true-peak limiter and a post-saturator level meter (and was reorganised underneath), and SatXComp and SatEQ make their first appearance.

| Plug-in | Version | What |
|---|---|---|
| SatComp | 0.5.2 | output limiter, post-sat meter, engine reorganised |
| SatXComp | 0.1.0 | new: 3-band multiband version of SatComp, with a post EQ |
| SatEQ | 0.1.0 | new: 5-band EQ with the waveshaper (series or parallel) |
| SatLim | 0.1.0 | new: saturator into an oversampled clipper and a true-peak limiter |

Each zip is named with its plug-in's own version. From now on a single plug-in update is released under its own tag (`satxcomp-v...`, `sateq-v...`) instead of bumping SatComp.

## New in SatComp
- **Output limiter (LIM button, on by default).** A true-peak brickwall at -0.2 dBTP for the end of the chain, so inter-sample peaks do not slip past a limiter downstream. 4x oversampled detection, 1.5 ms lookahead, program-dependent release. Its gain reduction is the `LIM` readout in the output column; the compressor GR number moved to the top of the GR meter. The switch is saved with presets and the project.
- **Constant extra latency of about 2.2 ms at 48 kHz**, reported to the host whether the limiter is on or off (so toggling never shifts the track).
- **Thin post-saturator level meter** between the saturator and compressor cells; the whole meter turns accent for 1 s when the saturator output passes 0 dBFS.
- **Signal flow** on the About page shows the limiter.
- **INIT starts with drive 0 and threshold 0.**
- **Better saturator (ASYM).** The even-order part of the waveshaper no longer drags DC and sub-bass pulses into the signal, so lopsided shapers and custom curves keep their grit with calmer peaks. The new ASYM slider under the plot sets how much of the even part each shaper keeps (stored per shaper, saved with presets and projects). INIT sounds slightly cleaner in the lows with Tube, Diode, Rectify, Asym Fold and Custom.
- **Limiter retune.** A hard clip at the ceiling in front of the gain stage takes tall peaks so the body is not ducked, and the release range is 15-80 ms.
- **SAT GAIN.** The saturator's OUT GAIN cell is renamed SAT GAIN (same parameter, presets unchanged).

## Changed
- **Engine split into blocks.** The saturator, compressor, sidechain key input and EQ stages are now separate, reusable pieces; the SatComp engine only wires them together. Rendering 101 test scenarios (every shaper and oversampling mode, compressor, EQ, sidechain, bypass, delta, gain match, automation, other sample rates) through v0.5.1 and v0.5.2 gives bit-identical audio and meter values (the limiter-era check shifts by the new latency; bypass, delta and sidechain-solo control timing is 2 ms earlier).
- **BYPASS is a plain bypass (SatComp and SatXComp).** The loudness-matched bypass of v0.3-0.5.1 is retired: it scaled the dry input to the processed RMS, which with a hot saturator and no limiter in the path could peak up to +10 dB. Bypass now returns the dry input at its own level, like SatLim's, and the BYP readout is gone. Bypass audio differs from v0.5.1; everything else renders identically.
- **Shared UI toolkit.** The drawing canvas, colour schemes, fonts and the slider/button widget kit moved into their own crate. A scripted 460-frame render of the editor (sliders, EQ page, About, Appearance, menus, scope, presets) drew identically to v0.5.1 before the limiter work changed the layout.
- The About > Changelog page has a v0.5.2 entry.

## Notes
- Parameter ids, presets, projects and `themes.json` are unchanged: a v0.5.1 preset or project loads the same, with the limiter switched on (it did not exist before). A v0.5.1 project will sound the same except for peaks above -0.2 dBTP, and the track is delayed by the new latency (compensated by the host).
- AU is shelved for now (no demand yet).
- CPU use is unchanged within measurement noise (about 0.3-0.7% of one core at 48 kHz); the limiter's interpolator only runs while the signal is loud enough to matter.
