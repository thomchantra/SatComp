First public build of SatComp: a waveshaping saturator with a compressor and a 2-stage 3-band EQ, in a black-and-white monospace UI. VST3 and CLAP for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel) and Windows.

## What is in it
- **Saturator:** 15 shapers including a Custom one you draw yourself (pencil icon, up to 10 points), drive, bias, HP/LP split, tone, sat mix, output gain, 1x/2x/4x oversampling.
- **Compressor:** threshold, ratio, attack, release, knee and knee shape, peak/RMS detector, low cut, lookahead, feed-forward/feedback blend, GR distortion, makeup and mix.
- **EQ:** two stages (pre and post compressor), three bands each plus tilt, with a live spectrum.
- **Metering and tools:** output meter, gain-reduction meter, GAIN MATCH, DELTA monitor, loudness-matched BYPASS.
- **Presets:** save and load your own, step through them, six Drums factory presets. A name wrapped in asterisks (`*NAME*`) means the preset has been modified.
- **20 colour schemes**, a resizable window, and an About page (click the title) with the signal flow and the changelog.

## Changes since the 0.4 build
- Fixed the PK readout and peak reset after reopening the window.

## Install
Download the zip for your system below, copy the bundles to your plug-in folders (see the README) and rescan. The macOS build is not notarized: if Gatekeeper blocks it, run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine` on the bundle. The Windows build has only had light testing.
