## New: SatDiff 0.1.0
A waveshaper saturator with phase colouring effects. Dedicated to sweet saturated sounds.
- **Two complete saturators, A and B,** each with its own shaper, drawable curve (up to 30 points, with handles that bend a segment), tile, drive, bias, HPF / LPF, tone and ASYM. **MORPH** blends them: a static crossfade, or a compressor-style detector that moves the blend with the input level (shape A on silence, shape B on loud peaks), with DEPTH and SPEED.
- **TILE** folds or repeats the curve so BIAS walks through copies of it.
- **PHASE** rotator and a colour slot, before or after the saturators: diffusor, phaser, flanger, ring mod or frequency shifter, with COLOR MIX.
- **Output:** a 24 dB per octave high-pass with mid / side switches (side only is a mono sub), IN and OUT gain with clip lamps, a true-peak limiter, level match on the SAT readout, a scope / spectrum meter, plain BYPASS and eight factory presets.

## All plug-ins
- **Draw view takes 20 points** (was 10) and gets an **EVEN** tool next to ODD that mirrors the drawn half across the Y axis.
- **Buttons and switches light up under the pointer.** The header has a redrawn icon with the version next to the name.
- **Much lighter on the CPU.** The saturator's oversampling filters were rewritten, odd shapers skip work they do not need, and SatXComp skips idle stages: at default settings SatXComp went from about 1.9 % to 0.6 % of a core.
- **Linux:** the meters and the scope scroll steadily regardless of the host's block size, UI writes show at once, and a hang of the whole host UI when plug-in windows are opened and closed is fixed. Still experimental: X11 only.

## SatComp 0.5.3
- **Compressor plot:** the **CEIL** and **FLOOR** labels in its lower right rescale it (ceiling up to +18 dB, floor down to -72 dB), so a signal that clips shows past a dashed 0 dB line. dB legends, a dashed line from the threshold handle, and a draggable curve: the line above the threshold sets the ratio, the line below it the threshold. The ratio handle no longer hides under the transient-meter icon at 1:1. The range is saved with the project.

## Install and manuals
- **All-in-one downloads:** `SatComp-Suite-v0.5.3-<platform>.zip` holds all four plug-ins (VST3 and CLAP) and one joint manual, with one `INSTALL.txt`; the joint manual is also attached as `SatComp-Suite-Manual-v0.5.3.pdf`. On macOS there is also `SatComp-Suite-v0.5.3-macOS-universal.pkg`, an installer for all users (not notarized: right-click it and choose Open if macOS objects).
- **Manuals** are redrawn with a signal-flow chart and an annotated overview of each plug-in, and SatDiff has one too (PDF in each zip, and under OPEN MANUAL on the About tab).
- SatComp's manual had the pre-EQ drawn before the saturator; the chain is saturator, pre EQ, compressor, mix, post EQ, output gain, limiter.
